# 问答准备

5 分钟讲完后可能被问 1–2 个问题。下面按"最可能被问"排序。

**原则**：知道就答，不知道就说不知道。老师给的是口头反馈，**承认边界比硬编答案得分高得多**。

---

## 很可能问到

### Q：为什么不干脆上更大的电池就好？
> Because the bottleneck isn't how much you store, it's how fast you can deliver it. Those are different specifications. A bigger battery gives you more energy, not a faster response — the response time is set by the power electronics and the chemistry, not by capacity.

补充（如果还有时间）：而且我们查到的那座混合电站里，超级电容的功率（58 MW）**比电池还大**（42 MW），正说明买它买的是功率不是容量。

### Q：快慢怎么分？控制器具体怎么做？
> In the simplest form it's a filter. You take the power the grid is asking for, low-pass it, and send the slow part to the battery. Whatever is left — the fast part — goes to the supercapacitor. The filter time constant is the design knob: make it slower and you protect the battery more, but you need a bigger capacitor.

这条答得好会加分，因为它点出了**设计权衡**，不只是描述。

### Q：超级电容每度电那么贵，划算吗？
> Per kilowatt-hour it's far more expensive than a battery — so you never buy it for energy. You buy it for two things: power per kilogram, and cycle life. It takes about a million cycles where a battery takes a thousand. In a job that cycles constantly, that's where it pays back.

### Q：既然有快速控制了，惯量还重要吗？
**这是最好的问题，答好了很加分：**
> They're not the same thing. Inertia is instantaneous and needs no measurement — it's just physics resisting change. A controller has to *measure* the frequency first, then decide, then act. However fast you make it, there's always a detection delay in front. So fast control substitutes for inertia, but it doesn't become inertia.

### Q：这种电池+超容的混合，现在普遍吗？
**老实回答：**
> At grid scale, no — it's still rare. We found essentially one plant of this size, in Shanxi, connected in August 2025. Supercapacitors are already very common in faster, smaller roles: wind turbine pitch systems, port cranes, rail regenerative braking, industrial UPS. The grid-scale version is new.

承认"还很少"比吹成行业标配可信得多。

## 可能问到

### Q：Hornsdale 那 100 毫秒是实测的吗？
> The 100 millisecond figure is from Aurecon's independent impact study — it's the fast-frequency-response design figure, and a lab test measured about 134 milliseconds. For a real event, we have the 25th of August 2018 interconnector trip: the battery swung from charging at 37 megawatts to delivering 84, and South Australia needed no load shedding.

⚠️ **不要**把 100 ms 和 2017 年 12 月 Loy Yang 那次事故绑在一起——那个说法出自媒体报道，而且原文自己写明 AEMO 的数据采样太慢、没测到。

### Q：那 1.16 亿澳元是怎么算出来的？
> It's Aurecon's modelled counterfactual — what the frequency-control markets would have cost in 2019 if Hornsdale hadn't been there. About 80 million of it is contingency services and 36 million regulation.

### Q：你们这是哪一部分的课程内容？
> Mostly Part A — it's a closed loop with an error signal, a controller and an actuator. The thing we found interesting is that almost all the engineering difficulty is in the actuator's bandwidth, not in the control law.

## 如果被问住了

直接说：

> That's a good question — we didn't look into that, so I'd rather not guess.

或者：

> I don't know the number for that. What I can tell you is [讲一个你确实有出处的相关事实].

**不要**现编数字。这场不计分，但编数字被当场追问会很难看。
