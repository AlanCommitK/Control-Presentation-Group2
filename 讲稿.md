## 半场 A

### 页 1 · Britain lost 1,481 MW in a second （0:23）

Ninth of August, twenty-nineteen. Lightning hits a line north of London.

▶ Within **one second**, Britain lost **1,481 MW**.

▶ The grid is planned around one loss of **1,000 MW**.

▶ Frequency crossed **48.8 Hz**; **931 MW** of demand was cut.

### 页 2 · A third of it tripped itself （0:21）

A steam unit and a wind farm are most of that.

▶ But **150 MW** of small generators tripped on vector shift,

▶ and **350 MW** on rate of change of frequency — **a third**, switching itself off.

### 页 3 · 50 Hz is a set point （0:22）

Put that in control terms. **50 Hz** is a set point; the error is the deviation from it; the plant is the whole grid.

▶ The statutory band is **49.5–50.5 Hz**, and load change is the disturbance.

### 页 4 · The plant is a water tank （0:26）

What does that plant look like? You have built it already. A tank: capacitance times rate of change of pressure equals inflow minus outflow.

▶ The grid is the same equation — inertia is the capacitance, damping is one over R, time constant **10 seconds**.

### 页 5 · Droop is a proportional gain （0:22）

Primary control is proportional. Each responding generator moves output in proportion to the frequency error. That is the whole controller.

▶ The Grid Code makes each unit settable from **3% to 5%**. At **4%**, the loop gain is **25**.

### 页 6 · 1/(1+L) never reaches zero （0:21）

With no droop, a load step moves frequency **5.00 Hz**.

▶ With **4%** droop, **0.19 Hz**.

▶ That is one over one plus L, **1/26** — never zero. Secondary control removes the rest.

## 半场 B

### 页 7 · A real actuator adds a pole （0:19）

Thank you. So far the actuator was instant. Real ones are not.

▶ Add one lag — **200 ms** for a battery, **2.7 s** for a turbine — and the loop is second order.

### 页 8 · Gain cannot move the line （0:25）

Here is the root locus. Two real poles move toward each other, meet, and the branches turn vertical.

▶ After that the real part is fixed. Gain only slides the poles along that line. The battery breaks away at **−2.55**, the turbine at **−0.235**.

### 页 9 · A third pole bends it right （0:18）

That vertical branch assumes two poles.

▶ Add a measurement filter and it does bend right.

▶ The limits are **155** and **133**; we use **25**. The damping ratio fails first.

### 页 10 · The actuator sets the depth （0:21）

Same grid, same **4%** droop, same **0.1 pu** step. The dashed line is the statutory floor.

▶ The turbine falls to **49.48 Hz**.

▶ The battery holds **49.79 Hz** — **2.5×** shallower, and only the actuator changed.

### 页 11 · Faster is not instant （0:20）

Faster is not instant.

▶ In a real trip the machine peaked at **360 ms**, the grid-forming battery at **840 ms**.

▶ Within **200 ms**: **13.7%** of the machine's two-second total, **2.0%** of the battery's.

### 页 12 · Inertia adds no pole （0:14）

Because inertia is not a loop.

▶ It measures nothing, so it adds no pole. Anything that measures adds one; grid-forming tries not to.

### 页 13 · The tank is shrinking （0:18）

And the tank is shrinking.

▶ At **H = 2 s** the initial rate of change is **1.25 Hz/s**,

▶ above even the relaxed relay setting,

▶ and the turbine dips to **49.24 Hz**.

### 页 14 · Move the pole, not the gain （0:12）

You cannot answer that with gain.

▶ You move the pole.

▶ **1 second** for batteries, **5 ms** for grid-forming. Thank you.
